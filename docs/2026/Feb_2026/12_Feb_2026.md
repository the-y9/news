## Health
#### Headline: WEF’s Compact on Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR) 2025
  - The **World Economic Forum (WEF)** launched the **Compact on Antimicrobial Resistance 2025**, supported by the **Unified Coalition for AMR Responses (UKARE)**.
  - **UKARE** is part of the Quadripartite Joint Secretariat on AMR, involving UN bodies like **WHO** and **UNEP**.
  - **Global Antibiotic Resistance Surveillance Report 2025** (WHO) highlights:
    - India suffered **1.27 million deaths** in 2019 due to AMR.
    - Global economic impact is estimated between **$3 trillion to $5 trillion**.
  - AMR occurs when pathogens (bacteria, fungi, viruses) no longer respond to medicines due to genetic changes, often accelerated by **over-the-counter medication**, incomplete doses, and antibiotic use in **livestock farming**.
  - Key Initiatives:
    - **National Action Plan on AMR 2.0 (2025-2029)**: A comprehensive approach across human, animal, and environmental sectors.
    - **Nafithromycin**: India’s first indigenous antibiotic (2024) to treat drug-resistant bacteria.
    - **Global Antimicrobial Resistance Surveillance System (GLASS)**: A WHO data-sharing initiative.
    - **AWaRe (Access, Watch, Reserve)**: A WHO tool for antibiotic categorization.
    - **Red Line Awareness Campaign**: Marking antibiotic strips with a red line to prevent misuse.
    - **Operation AMRIT**: A Kerala government initiative to curb AMR.

## Environment
#### Headline: NITI Aayog Report: Scenarios Towards Viksit Bharat and Net Zero Waste
  - Identifies pathways for India’s waste sector to align with the **2070 Net Zero goals**.
  - Legal Classification: Waste in India is categorized into six types: **Municipal, Hazardous, Electronic, Biomedical, Plastic, and Construction**.
  - **Solid Waste Management Rules 2026**: Will replace the 2016 rules; introduces **four-stage segregation** (Wet, Dry, Sanitary, and Critical).
  - Challenges: Waste accounts for 2.5% of total emissions; only 39% of waste is scientifically processed.
  - Recommendations:
    - **Biomethanization** via biogas plants.
    - **Bioremediation**: Cleaning "legacy waste" (old dump sites) using microorganisms.
    - **Mission LiFE**: Promoting sustainable lifestyles to reduce generation.
    - Integration of **informal waste pickers** into the formal value chain.
    - Use of **Green Bonds** and **Carbon Credits** for financing.

## Economy
#### Headline: Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) Oct-Dec 2025
  - Conducted by the **National Statistical Office (NSO)** under **MoSPI**.
  - Key Definitions (measured per 1000 persons):
    - **LFPR**: People working or seeking work.
    - **WPR**: People actually employed.
    - **Unemployment Rate (UR)**: Percentage of the labor force that is unemployed.
  - Findings (Age 15+):
    - **LFPR** increased to 55.8%; **Female LFPR** rose to 34.9%.
    - **Unemployment Rate**: Declined to 4% (Rural) and 6.7% (Urban).
    - **Sectoral Concentration**: 58.5% of rural workforce in **Agriculture**; 61.9% of urban workforce in the **Tertiary sector**.

## Agriculture
#### Headline: NITI Aayog Study on Agriculture Transition and Net Zero
  - Agriculture contributes 14% to **Gross Value Added (GVA)** and 14% to national greenhouse gas emissions.
  - Issues: 86% of farmers are **small and marginal** (holding <2 hectares); high methane emissions from livestock and rice cultivation.
  - Strategic Sequencing:
    - **Crop Diversification**: Moving away from water-intensive rice and sugarcane.
    - **Resource Efficiency**: Promoting **Micro-irrigation** and solar/electric pumps to reduce conventional energy use.
  - Schemes: **PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana**, **National Mission on Natural Farming**, and **Bringing Green Revolution to Eastern India (BGREI)**.

## History
#### Headline: Birth Anniversary of Swami Dayanand Saraswati
  - Founded **Arya Samaj** in 1875; a social reformer who opposed child marriage and supported widow remarriage.
  - Famous Slogan: **"Back to Vedas"**.
  - Literary Work: **Satyarth Prakash**.
  - Philosophy: Propounded **Traitavada** (the eternal existence of Ishwara, Jeeva, and Prakriti).
  - Promoted the **Shuddhi Movement** and visualized a decentralized polity with the village as the primary unit.

#### Headline: Tamil Brahmi Script Identified in Egypt
  - Researchers found **Tamil Brahmi** inscriptions in Egypt's Valley of the Kings (1st–3rd Century AD).
  - Highlights ancient trade links between South India and Egypt.
  - Script was used to write ancient Tamil (300 BC – 100 AD); the **Thirukkural** is a major work in this script.

## Polity
#### Headline: Concept of "Right to Recall"
  - A form of **direct democracy** discussed in Rajya Sabha to allow voters to remove an elected representative before their term ends.
  - Status: Currently exists only at some local levels in India (e.g., **Chhattisgarh**).
  - Other direct democracy tools: **Referendum**, **Initiative**, and **Plebiscite**.

#### Headline: Guidelines for Senior Advocates Designation
  - **Supreme Court** issued new guidelines under the **Advocates Act 1961**.
  - Designation is a title, not a post, decided by a committee led by the **Chief Justice of India (CJI)**.
  - Eligibility: Minimum 10 years experience; age 45 years (usually).
  - Restrictions: Cannot file **Vakalatnama**, draft pleadings directly, or accept briefs without a junior advocate.

#### Headline: MHA Guidelines on Vande Mataram Protocols
  - **Vande Mataram** (National Song) was composed by **Bankim Chandra Chatterjee** in the novel **Anandamath**.
  - First sung at the **1896 INC Session** by Rabindranath Tagore.
  - Guidelines: Must be sung before the National Anthem; audience must stand (except during films/documentaries).
